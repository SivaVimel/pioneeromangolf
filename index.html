Overview

A platform, not a
single vehicle.

Derived from the Latin velox — swift, fast and agile — VELOX represents a new generation of electric mobility, combining modern design, dependable performance, comfort and efficiency.

The range of golf carts and Neighborhood Electric Vehicles is engineered to deliver practical mobility across diverse environments. The same electric platform is configured for the turf, the resort driveway, the campus loop and the warehouse floor — so a single supplier and a single service relationship covers all of them.

Modern design

Clean bodywork, cushioned upholstery and lighting that suits a five-star forecourt as readily as a service yard.

Dependable performance

Built for continuous daily duty, with parts and technical support held locally by Pioneer in Muscat.

Comfort

Quiet running, a settled ride and a full canopy — the difference between transport and a good arrival.

Efficiency

Zero tailpipe emissions, overnight charging and far fewer moving parts to maintain than a combustion equivalent.

Designed for every application

Where Velox works.

Six environments

Golf courses

Turf-friendly, comfortable and reliable mobility.

A two-seat car built around the round.

Wide, low-pressure tyres spread the load so maintained turf recovers behind the car, and the electric drivetrain keeps the course as quiet as it found it. A rear rack takes two full bags, and the fleet charges overnight rather than queuing for fuel.

  • 2 seats
  • Twin bag rack
  • Turf-rated tyres

Resorts & hotels

Quiet, elegant transport that lifts the guest experience.

Six seats for arrivals, villa runs and transfers.

Quiet enough to hold a conversation in from the lobby to the room. Cushioned upholstery, polished trim and a clean white body are specified to sit comfortably in front of a five-star entrance — and to keep looking that way through a full season.

  • 6 seats
  • Full canopy
  • Guest transfer

Tourism & lifestyle destinations

Seamless movement across expansive developments.

Distances too far to walk, too fine-grained to drive.

Integrated resorts, waterfronts and heritage sites are built at a scale that defeats visitors on foot. VELOX runs the promenades and internal roads on a single charge cycle, open-sided so the destination stays in view for the whole journey.

  • 6 seats
  • Extended routes
  • Open-sided

Campuses & facilities

Efficient transport for people and daily operations.

One vehicle covering both jobs on site.

Universities, hospitals, business parks and airports run the same two errands all day: move people between buildings, and move the staff who keep those buildings working. A six-seat shuttle handles both on fixed loops and charges between shifts.

  • 6 seats
  • Fixed shuttle loops
  • Between-shift charging

Industrial & utility

Robust vehicles built for demanding environments.

A working platform with a load bed.

A steel drop-side bed, front brush guard and all-terrain tyres put the same platform to work in warehouses, plants, ports and yards. Because it emits nothing at the tailpipe, it stays in service indoors — where a combustion utility vehicle cannot follow.

  • Drop-side cargo bed
  • Brush guard
  • All-terrain tyres

Residential & recreational

Safe, stylish and convenient personal mobility.

The short trips that don’t warrant a car.

In gated communities and coastal developments, a four-seat NEV covers the school gate, the clubhouse and the beach path. A lifted stance and road lighting suit community streets, and it charges from an ordinary household supply overnight.

  • 4 seats
  • Lifted suspension
  • Road lighting

The range

Four configurations.

Every VELOX shares the same electric platform and the same service parts. What changes is the body above it — seating, load space and stance — so a mixed fleet stays simple to maintain.
